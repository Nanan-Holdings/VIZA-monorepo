VIZA site redesign

Your Framer design becomes the look of the whole marketing site. The destination gallery you like stays and gets room to breathe. Every destination becomes a real, indexable page built for search.

The direction in one line: a calm, editorial travel brand that reads like a trustworthy consulate office, with the destination photo as the hero and the visa facts set out like the fields on a visa label.

Why it fixes the "scam" feel: the current site stacks badges on every card (快速通道, 保证送达, VIZA, 免费) in a tight grid. Sales noise is what makes a free service look suspicious. The new site says less, shows the real facts plainly, and lets whitespace and photography carry the trust.

Fixes first

Found while checking the live site today. These hold back search more than any design change, so they ship first.

critical

English pages never reach Google

Every visitor without a language cookie, Googlebot included, is sent with a 307 from any English URL to /zh-CN. 27 of the 38 URLs in the sitemap are redirects. Fix: serve each URL in its own language with hreflang and x-default, and offer the switch in a small bar instead of forcing it.

critical

Destination pages are invisible

/zh-CN/visa/japan renders on the client, has the site-wide title "VIZA — AI-powered visa applications" and an English description, and none of the 16 destinations is in the sitemap. These are the pages that should bring most traffic.

high

Chinese content is thin

All 13 blog posts are English only and /zh-CN/blog is the only Chinese blog URL. Chinese guides and news are the content gap for the Phase 1 market.

SEO pipeline

The same steps as the LUCA Robotics and Nanan Auto sites, run for VIZA.

  1. Keyword researchSemrush Keyword Magic Tool in the Kelin Chrome profile (Singapore database, English and Chinese queries), plus Google Keyword Planner. Output: docs/seo/keywords.csv and keyword-clusters.md with one primary and several secondary keywords per page. The Semrush API has no units left and the Ryze plan has ended, so this goes through the web UI as it did for Nanan Auto.
  2. Page mapClusters decide which destination, visa type and guide pages exist. Zero-volume pages are built only when they help someone who already landed.
  3. Content to template lengthSemrush SEO Content Template per target keyword sets the length, related terms and questions. Copy rewritten in Chinese first, then English.
  4. Technical layerServer rendered pages, per-page titles and descriptions, hreflang with x-default, sitemap with every destination, schema (Organization, TravelAgency with the Pasir Panjang address, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage), llms.txt, a 1200x630 link preview per page.
  5. Checks in the buildA copy check that blocks dashes, unsupported claims ("guaranteed approval", "official") and over-long titles, and a verify script that fetches every route as Googlebot and WhatsApp.
  6. Site Audit and trackingSemrush Site Audit after launch, fixes in one follow-up PR. Search Console verified for the domain, sitemap submitted, GA4 and Clarity (both already on the site) checked.

Build

  1. Scrape the Framer fileReuse nanan-auto/reference/scrape.mjs: SSR HTML, CSS, screenshots per section at 1440, 1000 and 390, computed styles.
  2. Design systemdocs/design-system/ANALYSIS.md with each Framer value next to the VIZA value, then tokens.css, base styles and section components inside the existing Next.js 16 marketing site, wired into its Tailwind config.
  3. PagesHome, destinations, destination template and how it works first; the rest restyled on the same sections. Existing data, next-intl copy and the portal apply flow stay.
  4. ShipScreenshots at desktop and phone for review, then merge and deploy. The repo is on the Viza Hobby Vercel team, which can't build the private repo from a push, so deploys go out with the VIZA Vercel token.

Decisions

  1. Approve this direction, or tell me what to push harder (more photography, darker, more playful).
  2. Who should search find first: anyone in Singapore applying for a visa, or Chinese nationals in Singapore? This sets whether Chinese sits at / and which keywords lead. My pick: Chinese at /, English at /en.
  3. Launch domain: stay on viza.it.com, or move to viza.sg (the Framer footer says VIZA.sg) before the SEO work? Moving later throws away rankings.
  4. Any real numbers or client quotes we can show? Until then those sections stay out.
