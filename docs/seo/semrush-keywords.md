# Semrush keyword research for VIZA (viza.it.com)

Pulled 25 Nov 2026 from the Semrush web UI (Keyword Magic Tool, broad match, database Singapore db=sg; Organic Research for domains), signed in as the team Semrush account in Edward's Chrome. No API units or MCP connector used.

How to read it: Volume is Semrush's monthly estimate for Singapore. Volumes shown as "2.9K" on screen are written here as rounded numbers (2,900). KD % is Semrush keyword difficulty ("n/a" means Semrush has not refreshed metrics for that row, so the volume is a rough floor). CPC is USD. Intent: I informational, N navigational, C commercial, T transactional. Only the top rows per seed that are relevant (visa intent, from Singapore) are kept. Full machine-readable list: `keywords.csv` (seed, language, volume, kd, cpc).

Method notes:
- "korea k-eta" returned no rows (the hyphen breaks the match). The seed "k-eta" was used instead and returned one real row.
- Semrush db=sg splits Simplified Chinese into separate words ("中国 签证 中心"). Seeds typed without spaces (新加坡签证, 日本签证 and so on) returned only zero-volume junk, so the spaced form (新加坡 签证) is what is recorded below. Chinese data in db=sg is thin: most rows are 20 to 30 searches a month, only a handful pass 100.
- Some rows have a shifted column layout on screen (extra intent letter) and were left out rather than guessed.

## What this says

- The volume in Singapore is arrival cards and e-visas (MDAC Malaysia 22,200, K-ETA 3,600, Indonesia visa on arrival 3,600, e visa India 1,600, Vietnam e visa 1,900) and China visa terms (apply china visa in singapore 6,600, china visa application singapore 5,400). These are the biggest openings and most have KD 23 to 36.
- Japan (2,900), Australia (1,600), US (1,000 for application) and Thailand (1,000) are the next tier. "Agency" and "services" wording has almost no volume, so page titles should lead with country plus "visa" plus "Singapore".
- viza.it.com ranks for 7 SG keywords, all below position 10, with zero traffic. Competitors win with one strong page each: Atlys with a Singapore arrival card post (about 1.7K traffic), VisaHQ with country pages and Australia ETA pages.
- Chinese db=sg data is thin. Only China visa centre terms, 日本 签证 (390), 越南 签证 (480) and 美国 签证 (320) are meaningful.
