# VIZA pricing

Two ways to apply, both free. The government visa fee is separate and paid
directly to the issuing authority, never to VIZA.

| | Guided | Full-service |
|---|---|---|
| VIZA fee | Free | Free |
| Application form | You answer, with VIZA's AI as your guide | A consultant prepares the answers with you |
| Documents | A checklist for your situation | A consultant checks your documents |
| Appointment and interview | You book and attend | We go through likely questions with you |
| Government fee | Paid directly to the authority | Paid directly to the authority |
| Good for | Straightforward applications | Complex cases or a past refusal |

The government fee for each destination, where one is published, is on that
destination's page: <https://viza.it.com/visa>.

Start an application: <https://viza.it.com/apply>
